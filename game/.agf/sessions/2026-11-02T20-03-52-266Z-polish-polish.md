I reworked the visuals of all six critique areas. Type-check passes, `npm run validate` reports 36/36 levels solvable, and `npm run pitfalls` shows 0 errors and 0 warnings. I've only seen the result in a web preview at 360x740 and 360x640, where all six store scenes rendered. Nothing has run on a device yet. Game logic, levels and save data are unchanged; the only addition to saved data is an optional field for the daily bonus.

**What changed (critique items 1–10):**
- **Board (1, 2):** the wall border around each level is no longer drawn, so the paths fill the screen width. The board sits in a dark frame in the chapter's colour. Unpainted tiles are sunken, mid-dark slots; painted tiles are bright, glossy and raised, with small paint splashes at the edges. The three states stay distinct when squinting.
- **Chapter worlds (2, 10):** I made six illustrated backdrops with PIL (meadow, lake, lavender, canyon, twilight, peaks), because the existing ones were empty gradients. Each chapter also gets its own brush colour. The backdrop drifts slowly with floating paint specks, and the board tilts down into view as its tiles pop in at level start.
- **Mechanics (3):**
  - Switches are big coloured key plates; shut doors are coloured blocks with a padlock.
  - Each switch and its doors pulse together in the same colour, and doors burst open when unlocked.
  - A letter tag stays on each for colourblind players.
  - The first time a mechanic appears, its tiles are ringed until the first move, and the level tip springs up with an icon.
- **Brush and feel (4):** the brush is bigger, leaves wet paint under its tip and a shimmering trail. Each newly painted tile plays a note that climbs in pitch, with a light haptic tick. New sounds for unlocking and stars.
- **Win (5):** light rays behind gold star images that pop in with chimes, a "Par beaten!" / "Perfect par!" banner, the brush dancing, and the existing coin fly and confetti.
- **HUD and type (6, 9):** dark glossy panels instead of white pills. Fill is a paint-bucket meter; moves vs par is a bar with three star pips. Undo/Reset/Hint/Settings/Back are raised 3D buttons with real icons. Numbers use the title font with a shadow, and labels are larger.
- **Home (7):** the hero board's brush now paints the chapter picture tile by tile in a loop. The chapter card shows that picture's progress. I added a **Daily Paint** button: once 3 levels are painted, one painted level comes back each day and its first clear pays 50 coins.
- **Gallery (8):** the Levels screen is now a gallery. Each chapter is a painting cut into six pieces, one per level. Clearing a level reveals its piece, and the next level's piece glows. It opens on the chapter in progress.
