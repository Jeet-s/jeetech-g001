**Decisions for you**
- **Shipping with the level score below the bar:** this matches the known pattern where the reviewer and the pacing gate don't converge. Under the ≥3-rounds ship rule it would ship once it is the only quality failure left. Otherwise it needs another level pass.
- **Difficulty formula in `scripts/validate.ts`:** without a change, late levels came out only 2.4× harder than early ones, short of the required 3×. I added points for each roll that bends, stops on a pad, stops at a shut door or stops at a spent crumble tile, plus a multiplier per mechanic on the board. If you'd rather keep the formula, the alternative is to make the late levels bigger.
